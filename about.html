About

Telos Research Group builds a small portfolio of independent products, tools, and research, each one standing entirely on its own.

We are not a single product with features bolted on over time. Every app in the portfolio is a separate repository with its own history, dependencies, deploy, and lifecycle — hunting intelligence, podcast generation, knowledge-graph extraction, fleet tooling. They share conventions and a build method. They share no code.

That is a deliberate constraint, not an accident of growth. A shared runtime package would turn a handful of standalone products into one distributed monolith that cannot be sold, killed, or handed off separately. So when two apps need the same thing, we copy it. Duplication across apps is cheaper than the coupling that removes the option to walk away from any one of them.

What we do share is how the work gets built — an agent fleet that operates on the apps and ships inside none of them. It is the reason a portfolio this wide is maintainable by a group this small.

We expand the portfolio when a problem is worth owning end to end, and we retire from it without ceremony when one isn't.

Telos Research Group is the working name for an entity still being formed. The domain telosrg.com and the GitHub organization github.com/TelosRG are held; the LLC itself is not yet registered.
